How to Play MathBlitz

⚡ Scoring & Streaks

Each correct answer awards 100 base points. Consecutive correct answers build your Streak Multiplier:

🔥 1-2 1.0x Multiplier
🔥 3-4 1.5x Multiplier
🔥 5-6 2.0x Multiplier
🔥 7+ 3.0x Multiplier

Answering quickly also grants up to +50 speed bonus points!

🎯 The 12 Medium Question Categories

1. Number Theory: Primes, tricky composites, divisibility tests, and factor counts.
2. Powers & Exponents: Base arithmetic, power laws, and evaluating expressions.
3. Missing Number in Equations: Solving for unknown terms in arithmetic equations.
4. Addition: Multi-digit and multi-term mental addition with carries.
5. HCF (GCD): Finding the greatest common divisor of positive integers.
6. LCM: Calculating lowest common multiples within mental math range.
7. Fractions: Addition, subtraction, multiplication, and reducing to lowest terms.
8. Missing Number in Series: Identifying arithmetic, geometric, and quadratic sequence rules.
9. Square Numbers: 2-digit squares, square roots, and consecutive square differences.
10. Calendar and Days: Modulo 7 day calculations, past/future dates, and day offsets.
11. Decimal Addition & Subtraction: Precise decimal calculations with alignment.
12. Decimal Multiplication & Division: Decimal place value shifts and mental division.
